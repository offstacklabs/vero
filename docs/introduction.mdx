# Welcome to Vero 🚀

Vero is an enterprise-grade, **open-source headless analytics agent framework** built for data engineering teams, product analysts, and developers.

Instead of paying $100k+/year for rigid legacy BI platforms (Looker, Tableau) or spinning up heavy caching proxy clusters (Cube.js), Vero gives you an **autonomous AI reasoning agent** that compiles plain English questions directly into verified, highly-optimized SQL for your warehouse.

## ⚡ Core Value Propositions

### 1. Zero-Middleware Architecture (Cube without the Cluster)
Traditional semantic tools require deploying and maintaining a massive proxy cluster (Node.js workers, Redis caching, and Cube Store pre-aggregations). 
Vero eliminates this entirely: queries run directly against your cloud data warehouse with **embedded AST query caching** ($0 compute, 0ms latency) backed by embedded DuckDB.

### 2. Context Engineering over YAML Bureaucracy
Stop writing 80 lines of nested YAML for basic metrics. With Vero:
- **Formulas are pure SQL**: Defined in a lean `metrics.yaml` file (`SUM(amount)`).
- **Rules are plain English**: Complex business guidelines and organizational exceptions live in `RULES.md` (*"Our fiscal year starts in February. Exclude test accounts with @internal.com"*).
- The LLM dynamically reconciles rules and formulas at runtime.

### 3. Pre-Execution Cost Guardrails
AI agents without cost controls will bankrupt your data team with accidental full-table scans. 
Vero executes native **BigQuery dry runs (`QueryJobConfig(dry_run=True)`)** before every query to calculate the exact scan byte volume and projected dollar cost at **$0 compute charges**. If a query exceeds your configured limit (e.g. 10 GB), Vero blocks execution and self-heals by injecting partition filters.

### 4. 100% In-VPC Privacy & Sovereignty
No customer data, warehouse credentials, or schemas ever leave your cloud perimeter. Vero runs completely inside your own infrastructure.
