# Snowflake

Vero connects to Snowflake using standard SQLAlchemy URLs and models query compute cost based on your warehouse size.

---

## Configuration

In your `.vero/config.yaml`:

```yaml
version: "1.0"
database:
  dialect: "snowflake"
  connection_string: "snowflake://<user>:<password>@<account>/<database>/<schema>?warehouse=<warehouse>&role=<role>"
cost_guard:
  rates:
    snowflake_credit_usd: 3.00
    snowflake_warehouse_size: "X-Small"
```

---

## Compute Cost Calculation

Snowflake charges by virtual warehouse credits per hour. Vero calculates execution compute as:

$$\text{Cost} = \left(\frac{\text{duration\_seconds}}{3600}\right) \times \text{credits\_per\_hour} \times \$3.00$$

* **X-Small**: 1 credit/hour
* **Small**: 2 credits/hour
* **Medium**: 4 credits/hour
* **Large**: 8 credits/hour
